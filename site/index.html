What's inside

Every run is different: random waves, a gamble on every egg, and bosses that fight back. Plays in English, French, Spanish and German, with keyboard, mouse, touch or a controller.

13Biomes

From Whispering Wood to The Void, each with its own monsters, weather and music.

12Bosses

A freezing Frost Bear, a splitting Maw, an Oni that smashes the ground, and more.

116Items

Common to Mythic, hatched from the magpie's eggs. Legendary and Mythic ones are earned with challenges.

29Crow skins

Each with its own passive, projectile, nest and caw.

∞Chaos Rift

Endless waves with mini bosses and 10 chaos levels. How far can you go?

26Spells

Meteors, chain lightning, a crow flock and more. Pick five, and unlock new slots as the run goes on.

Meet the bosses

Every boss has its own trick. Learn it, or lose your eggs.

Frost Bear

Caw to stop his roar, or mash to break out of the ice. At low HP he curls up and rolls away to heal.

The Maw

Its eye slows your feathers to a crawl, and when it's hurt it splits into two.

Oni

His kanabo slam sends a shockwave that breaks your spells. At half HP he flies into a rage.

Emperor Scorpion

Spits poison and vanishes into a sandstorm, circling the nest.

The Monkey King

Spinning bananas block your feathers. Break them to get through.

Rat King

Throws cheese at your nest, and rats pour out of it.
