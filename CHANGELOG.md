# Nestguard changelog

## 2.99.18 — Auto fire cooldown and coins that follow you

- Switching between auto fire and point-and-click now has a 1 second cooldown, whether you use the AUTO button, its key or a gamepad. A bar on the button drains while it recharges, and the button shakes if you press it too early.
- **Upgrades tab:** your coin count now follows you as you scroll. Once the one at the top scrolls out of view, a small coin counter stays in the top-right corner.
- **Egg screen:** when you open an egg, the other eggs in the nest no longer crack and shake behind it. They stay whole until your roll is over.
- **Item info without pausing:** the items on the left side of the screen no longer pause the game and open a pop-up when you click them. Hover over one (or tap it on a phone) to see a see-through bubble with its rarity, name and what it does, while the game keeps going.
- **More time before the next wave:** after you see what came out of your egg, the next wave now starts after 4.5 seconds (was 2.5). Tap or press Space to start it sooner.
- **Less teasing on the roll:** the reel sliding onto the better item next to yours and then back now happens about 1 roll in 11 (was about 1 in 3). Every other roll stops right on your item.
- **Lucky Charm** (Upgrades tab) now has **20 levels** at **+5%** egg luck each (was 10 levels at +12%), so it tops out at +100% instead of +120%. The first levels are cheaper (90 coins instead of 150). If you already bought some levels, you keep them, at +5% each.

## 2.99.17 — Ten ways to hatch

Five new ways for an egg to crack, on top of peck, rock, hop, spin and charge:
- **Heartbeat:** a thump-thump inside the egg, faster and faster, with the glow pulsing along.
- **Slam:** the egg flies up out of sight and crashes back down with a shockwave and a bounce.
- **Inflate:** the egg swells up like a balloon, wobbling and creaking, until it can't hold any more.
- **Zap:** lightning strikes the egg three times, cracking it more each time.
- **Waddle:** the egg waddles off to one side and back on its own, then trips and cracks.

## 2.99.16 — Webs on the ground, rarest item wins

- **Sticky Cobwebs** (and the Silk Crown) now spread a whole cobweb on the ground all around your nest, with dew drops glinting in it.
- **Wind Whisper** is now curling gusts of wind, both circling your crow and in its picture.
- **Frost Bear's Heart** gives your crow a glowing ice heart and icy wing tips instead of a white cape, and its picture is an ice heart. Big Lungs and Supernova Heart now glow as hearts too.
- **The rarest item wins:** items that sit on the same part of your crow no longer stack on top of each other. On the head (crests, crowns, tufts, horns), the eyes, the neck, the back (capes, quivers, drums) and the wing tips, only your rarest item shows.

## 2.99.15 — No skin crowns

- The Royal Crow, Eclipse Crow and Shardbearer skins no longer wear a gold crown. Crowns now only come from items (Thunder Crown, Rat King's Crown and so on).

## 2.99.14 — Wear your loot

Every item now shows. Your crow wears it, it circles around you, it sits on your nest or it changes your feathers:
- **On the crow:** metal, bone, glass or ember beak tips, the Thunder Crown, the Crown of Thorns and the Rook King's crown, crests (woodpeckers, hydras, the Eagle King), owl tufts, a monocle, glowing eyes, war paint, horns, halos, capes (Vampire Lord, Eternal Phoenix), coloured or gilded wing tips, scarves and neck charms, a quiver or a war drum on its back, a flaming tail and a glowing heart.
- **Around the crow:** pebbles, sparks, embers, a guardian feather, leaves, moths, a spell book, the Hourglass of Ages, the sun, the blood moon, the Moon Sickle, spell blades, little crows and a ghostly twin.
- **On the nest:** moss, gold coins, metal or bark bands, bones, amber resin, clover, acorns, berries, stones, icicles, dew, a golden egg, a small shrine, an Aegis ring and Titan shell plates.
- **On your feathers:** fire, ice, lightning, void or blood glows, needle tips and barbs. Feathers also grow a little as your damage goes up.
- **Equipping:** at the start of the next wave, a new item pops up above your crow and hovers, spinning in a glow of its rarity colour with sparkles circling it, while your crow shows a "!". Then it gets pulled down onto your crow and lands with a flash, a ring burst and its name. Its piece only appears on your crow once it lands.

- **New item pictures:** every item's icon now shows the piece it gives you (the crown, the cape, the coins, the quiver, the glowing feather, and so on), so you can tell what you'll be wearing.

Every item has its own look except the magpie ones, which already show as magpies. The boss items get their own as well: the Fairy Crown gives you a halo and fairy wings, Oni's Club gives you horns, the Dragon Scale gives you a dragon cape, and so on. When two items want the same spot, the rarer one wins.

## 2.99.13 — Five ways to hatch

Every egg now hatches in one of five ways, picked at random:
- **Peck:** something knocks from inside, slowly and then faster, until a beak pokes through.
- **Rock:** the egg rocks side to side, harder and harder and creaking, until it nearly tips over.
- **Hop:** the egg hops, a little higher each time, and lands with a thud and a squash.
- **Spin:** the egg twirls faster and faster with a rising whirr and lifts off the ground.
- **Charge:** the egg trembles, its glow swells and golden light shines out through the cracks.

The hatching lasts 0.3 seconds longer, so there's time to see it. You can still skip it with a tap or Space.

## 2.99.12 — A shinier egg roll

- **The egg drops in:** it falls into view, squashes as it lands and bounces back before the pecking starts.
- **The shell pops open:** when the egg bursts, its top flies off spinning, a beam of light shoots up and there's a whoosh as the reel starts.
- **The reel unrolls** from the middle, with light streaks while it spins fast.
- **Landing:** every other item dims, and a shine sweeps across the item you got. Rare and better items also send up a beam of light in their colour.
- **The reveal:** your item floats gently, with sparkles circling it. Rarer items get more sparkles, and mythic ones are rainbow.
- All of this uses only cheap animations, so the roll stays as smooth as in 2.99.11. The full-screen flash when the egg bursts is gone (the beam of light replaces it), and the reel is drawn in advance so it doesn't stutter when it appears.

## 2.99.11 — A livelier crow, no stretchy snakes

- **The crow comes alive:** it breathes, flicks its tail, ruffles its wings every few seconds and turns its head to glance around. While it caws, its head shakes.
- **No stretchy snakes:** cawing at a snake (or a gator, or the Magma Dragon's tail) used to shove its head away while its body stayed put, so it stretched out to about twice its length. The body now gets pulled along with the head.
- **Plain health bar:** the nest's health is plain red and its shield plain blue, with no fade or stripes.
- **Caw button:** the crow-head icon is gone. The button just says CAW!.

## 2.99.10 — Pecking eggs and a smoother roll

- **Smooth egg rolls again:** the new light rays were a huge spinning layer that made every egg roll drop frames. They're now smaller, painted once, and only shown while the egg shakes and when you see your item. The shockwave rings were redrawn every frame and now aren't, and the battlefield hidden behind the egg screen is no longer drawn. The roll runs as smoothly as it did before 2.99.9.
- **Eggs peck from inside:** eggs in the nest knock now and then with a little *tok*, hop and throw off bits of shell. Rarer and golden eggs are more restless, and hovering over an egg makes it peck.
- **A nicer roll:** the egg is pecked from inside, slowly at first and then faster and faster, until it cracks, a beak pokes through and it bursts. The reel overshoots a little and slides back. When it stops at the edge of an item, it can slide onto the better item next to it for a moment before settling back. Rays are soft for common items and brighter for rarer ones.

## 2.99.9 — Faster pace and a bigger egg roll

- **No more waiting after an egg:** once you see what you got, the next wave starts after 2.5 seconds (was 8). Tap anywhere or press Space to start it right away. The first monster now shows up almost as soon as the wave begins.
- **Busier early waves:** waves 1 to 11 bring up to 70% more monsters, coming in closer together. Each one has a bit less HP, so the early game is livelier without getting harder overall.
- **A more epic egg roll:** light rays build up while the egg shakes, it bursts with a flash and a shockwave, the needle kicks on every item that passes, and the prize lands with confetti in its rarity colour. Rare, epic, legendary and mythic items get more rings, more confetti and a little fanfare. The roll is also a bit shorter.
- **Golden eggs no longer lag the game:** the egg screen used to blur the whole battlefield every frame. It now covers it with a plain dark backdrop, and the paused battlefield isn't redrawn behind a golden egg. The shop's nest is also drawn once instead of several times a second.

## 2.99.8 — The Old Gator circles the nest

- After every bite, the Old Gator now dives and **circles around the nest underwater** (about half a lap, either way round), spitting frogs all around it, then surfaces and comes in again. He used to swim straight away from the nest.

## 2.99.7 — The Old Gator bites and runs

- **Bite and dive:** after every bite (including a death roll, whether it ends or you break it with a caw), the Old Gator sinks underwater, swims away from the nest and **spits frogs out behind him** for a few seconds. Then he swims back, surfaces and comes in again.
- **Fewer frogs on call:** his frog call now brings **1 frog** instead of 2.

## 2.99.6 — The Old Gator wakes up, and tougher challenges

### Old Gator rework
The boss of Murkwater Marsh finally gets moves of his own:
- **Death Roll:** after he surfaces from a dive, his next bite clamps onto the nest and rolls it, hurting it every half second for 2.5 seconds. **Caw to break free** (this works even while your caw is recharging) and stun him.
- **Young Again:** once, when he drops to half health, the shard he swallowed glows. He heals 15%, his hide hardens, he moves faster and a chorus of frogs answers his call.
- His monster index entry tells you how to deal with him.

### Challenges
- **Glass Nest:** the nest now has 33% of its HP (was 40%).
- **Swarm:** 80% more enemies (was 60%), and they move 25% faster (was 20%).
- **No Spells** is unchanged; it was already about right.
- Each challenge is now roughly a coin flip with the upgrades a world needs, and a sure win with a bit more. The Void's challenges stay beatable for a fully upgraded crow.

## 2.99.5 — The Big Balance Update

Every world, item, permanent upgrade and spell was tested with thousands of simulated runs and retuned.

### The short version
- **A steady climb.** Each world now asks for a bit more of your Roost upgrades than the one before: Whispering Wood can be won with none, and **The Void needs a fully upgraded crow**. No more sudden walls or worlds that are easier than the one before them.
- **The Void is the true final test**, and **Void Nightmare stays hard even when you have everything**.
- **"+% damage" and "+% attack speed" items now do what they say.** They used to get weaker the more you upgraded.
- Overpowered items, upgrades and spells were toned down and weak ones were made worth taking.

### Bug fixes
- Items that give "+X% damage" or "+X% attack speed" (Sharpened Quills, Quick Wings, War Drum, Blood Moon, Twin Crows and 16 more) only counted your crow's starting stats, so with upgrades they did almost nothing. They now use your real stats. Battle Rhythm is fixed the same way.
- Resetting your upgrades now gives back exactly what you paid, even after the price changes below.

### Worlds
- **Easier:** Frostfang Pass, Ashen Alleys, Sunscorch Dunes.
- **Harder:** Silkshade Hollow, Cinder Peak, Glimmer Glade (no longer easier than Cinder Peak), Blossom Ridge, The Void.
- **The Void** has its own difficulty steps: Hard +25% enemy HP / +15% damage, Expert +50% / +30%, Nightmare +110% / +60%. The difficulty picker shows them.
- Whispering Wood, Murkwater Marsh, Banana Jungle and the Chaos Rift are unchanged.

### Items
**Toned down**
- Twin Feathers is now **Epic** (was Rare), up to 3 copies (was 4).
- Storm Feathers is now **Legendary** (was Epic), up to 2 copies (was 3). Still unlocked from the start.
- Storm Caller: feathers bounce to 1 more enemy (was 2); stun chance 15% (was 10%).
- Hydra Quill: +2 feathers, no longer bounces.
- Monkey King's Staff: +1 feather and 1 bounce (was +2 and 2).
- Raven Legion: +2 feathers (was +3).

**Made stronger**
- Void Feathers: also +60% damage.
- Solar Flare: every 3 seconds (was 6), and the fire burns twice as hard.
- Thunder Crown: every second (was 2), up to 5 enemies (was 4), 4x damage (was 3x).
- Fairy Crown: spells recharge 45% faster (was 35%) and hit 80% harder (was 50%).
- Chaos Egg: 4 items from uncommon to epic (was 3 from common to rare).
- Gator's Jaw: +50% damage (was +25%).
- Aegis of Twigs: 150 shield every wave (was 60).
- Titan Shell: +500 nest HP (was +200), biters take 60 (was 40).
- Bastion Nest: +200 nest HP (was +120), 35% less damage (was 25%).
- Overcharge: 25% faster / 40% harder (was 15% / 20%).
- Crown of Thorns: 50 damage (was 30), bursts for 80% (was 50%).
- Moth Dust, Shiny Pebble, Twine Nest, Bone Marrow, Echo Stone and Pine Resin got small boosts.

### Roost upgrades
- **Second Feather** costs more (base 500, was 90): it was by far the best deal in the game.
- **Loud Voice** costs more (base 40, was 10).
- **Giant Slayer:** +15% per level (was +10%).
- **Spell Focus:** +15% spell damage per level (was +10%).
- **Mending Moss:** 1 HP per second per level (was 0.6).
- **Bramble Nest:** 15 damage per level (was 9).
- Already bought levels are kept, and resetting refunds what you actually paid.

### Spells
Expensive late spells are now clearly worth their price, and the strongest cheap ones were evened out.
- Faster recharge: Shadow Crow 22 s (was 30), Crow Flock 20 s (was 28), Black Hole 20 s (was 28), Starfall 20 s (was 28), Meteor Shower 19 s (was 26), Eruption 15 s (was 22), Plague Cloud 16 s (was 22), Hex Mark 15 s (was 20), Frost Ring 18 s (was 24), Whirlpool 17 s (was 22).
- More damage: Meteor Shower rocks hit about 40% harder, Black Hole's collapse about 40% harder.
- Toned down: Gale Burst recharges in 18 s (was 14); Thorn Wall recharges in 32 s (was 26), cuts less and slows less.
