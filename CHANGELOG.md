# Nestguard changelog

## 2.99.6 — Tougher challenges

- **Glass Nest:** the nest now has 33% of its HP (was 40%).
- **Swarm:** 80% more enemies (was 60%), and they move 25% faster (was 20%).
- **No Spells** is unchanged; it was already about right.
- Each challenge is now roughly a coin flip with the upgrades a world needs, and a sure win with a bit more. The Void's challenges stay beatable for a fully upgraded crow.

## 2.99.5 — The Big Balance Update

Every world, item, permanent upgrade and spell was tested with thousands of simulated runs and retuned.

### The short version
- **A steady climb.** Each world now asks for a bit more of your Roost upgrades than the one before: Whispering Wood can be won with none, and **The Void needs a fully upgraded crow**. No more sudden walls or worlds that are easier than the one before them.
- **The Void is the true final test**, and **Void Nightmare stays hard even when you have everything**.
- **"+% damage" and "+% attack speed" items now do what they say.** They used to get weaker the more you upgraded.
- Overpowered items, upgrades and spells were toned down and weak ones were made worth taking.

### Bug fixes
- Items that give "+X% damage" or "+X% attack speed" (Sharpened Quills, Quick Wings, War Drum, Blood Moon, Twin Crows and 16 more) only counted your crow's starting stats, so with upgrades they did almost nothing. They now use your real stats. Battle Rhythm is fixed the same way.
- Resetting your upgrades now gives back exactly what you paid, even after the price changes below.

### Worlds
- **Easier:** Frostfang Pass, Ashen Alleys, Sunscorch Dunes.
- **Harder:** Silkshade Hollow, Cinder Peak, Glimmer Glade (no longer easier than Cinder Peak), Blossom Ridge, The Void.
- **The Void** has its own difficulty steps: Hard +25% enemy HP / +15% damage, Expert +50% / +30%, Nightmare +110% / +60%. The difficulty picker shows them.
- Whispering Wood, Murkwater Marsh, Banana Jungle and the Chaos Rift are unchanged.

### Items
**Toned down**
- Twin Feathers is now **Epic** (was Rare), up to 3 copies (was 4).
- Storm Feathers is now **Legendary** (was Epic), up to 2 copies (was 3). Still unlocked from the start.
- Storm Caller: feathers bounce to 1 more enemy (was 2); stun chance 15% (was 10%).
- Hydra Quill: +2 feathers, no longer bounces.
- Monkey King's Staff: +1 feather and 1 bounce (was +2 and 2).
- Raven Legion: +2 feathers (was +3).

**Made stronger**
- Void Feathers: also +60% damage.
- Solar Flare: every 3 seconds (was 6), and the fire burns twice as hard.
- Thunder Crown: every second (was 2), up to 5 enemies (was 4), 4x damage (was 3x).
- Fairy Crown: spells recharge 45% faster (was 35%) and hit 80% harder (was 50%).
- Chaos Egg: 4 items from uncommon to epic (was 3 from common to rare).
- Gator's Jaw: +50% damage (was +25%).
- Aegis of Twigs: 150 shield every wave (was 60).
- Titan Shell: +500 nest HP (was +200), biters take 60 (was 40).
- Bastion Nest: +200 nest HP (was +120), 35% less damage (was 25%).
- Overcharge: 25% faster / 40% harder (was 15% / 20%).
- Crown of Thorns: 50 damage (was 30), bursts for 80% (was 50%).
- Moth Dust, Shiny Pebble, Twine Nest, Bone Marrow, Echo Stone and Pine Resin got small boosts.

### Roost upgrades
- **Second Feather** costs more (base 500, was 90): it was by far the best deal in the game.
- **Loud Voice** costs more (base 40, was 10).
- **Giant Slayer:** +15% per level (was +10%).
- **Spell Focus:** +15% spell damage per level (was +10%).
- **Mending Moss:** 1 HP per second per level (was 0.6).
- **Bramble Nest:** 15 damage per level (was 9).
- Already bought levels are kept, and resetting refunds what you actually paid.

### Spells
Expensive late spells are now clearly worth their price, and the strongest cheap ones were evened out.
- Faster recharge: Shadow Crow 22 s (was 30), Crow Flock 20 s (was 28), Black Hole 20 s (was 28), Starfall 20 s (was 28), Meteor Shower 19 s (was 26), Eruption 15 s (was 22), Plague Cloud 16 s (was 22), Hex Mark 15 s (was 20), Frost Ring 18 s (was 24), Whirlpool 17 s (was 22).
- More damage: Meteor Shower rocks hit about 40% harder, Black Hole's collapse about 40% harder.
- Toned down: Gale Burst recharges in 18 s (was 14); Thorn Wall recharges in 32 s (was 26), cuts less and slows less.
